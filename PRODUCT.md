# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KDE Plasma users who use Codex through a ChatGPT subscription and want to glance at remaining usage without opening another application.

## Product Purpose

Expose the current Codex allowance, reset time, reset inventory, and secondary model limits in a compact Plasma-native widget. Success means the panel indicator is immediately legible and the expanded view contains no redundant information.

## Brand Personality

Native, concise, dependable. The widget should feel like part of KDE Plasma while carrying a restrained Codex identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like density, decorative gradients, oversized branding, duplicated metrics, and custom controls that behave differently from adjacent Plasma widgets.

## Design Principles

1. Reuse native KDE components before introducing custom drawing.
2. Make the panel glance readable at its real rendered size.
3. Show each fact once and give operational information priority.
4. Keep Codex branding to a single purposeful accent.
5. Preserve clear loading and unavailable states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast at small panel sizes, do not rely on color alone for state, respect the Plasma system font and scaling, and keep all controls keyboard-accessible.
